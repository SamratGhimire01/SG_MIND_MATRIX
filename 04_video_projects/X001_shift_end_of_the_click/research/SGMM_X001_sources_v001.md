# X001 "The End of the Click" — sources (checked 2 Nov 2026)

Every number in the script and on screen comes from this list. Checked against the original publication; where the original is gated, the citing article is named.

## 1. Google Search traffic to publishers −40.2% (Jul 2025 → Jul 2026) · used in L04 + NOW screen
- **Cited article (checked):** Matt G. Southern, "Google Search Decline Steepened To 40% YoY For Publishers", Search Engine Journal, 24 Sep 2026 — https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-search-decline-publishers-chartbeat/590681/
  Wording: "Chartbeat reports a 40.2% year-over-year decline for Google Search across its publisher network from July 2025 to July 2026." Prior year: −21.9%. Google Search's share of network pageviews: ~9% (Jul 2024) → 5% (Jul 2026).
- **Original:** Chartbeat, *2026 Publisher Playbook* — https://lp.chartbeat.com/2026-publisher-playbook-chartbeat . The landing page shows no figures; the report is a download behind the page, so the 40.2% is quoted via SEJ.
- **Caveats (say "one study" on screen):** Chartbeat client sites only, no site count given, skews to news and media publishers; SEJ notes the playbook doesn't say whether the decline is in pageviews or in share of pageviews. Chartbeat sells analytics to publishers.

## 2. Condé Nast: "plan as if search is zero" · used in L05
- **Quote (checked):** Rob Tornoe, "Approaching Google Zero: As search referrals plunge, news publishers must anticipate they'll never rebound", Editor & Publisher, 30 Jul 2026 — https://www.editorandpublisher.com/stories/approaching-google-zero-as-search-referrals-plunge-news-publishers-must-anticipate-theyll,262684
  Roger Lynch (Condé Nast CEO) on TBPN: "Last year, I told our teams: assume there's no search. You have to have your businesses planned as if search is zero." and "We don't expect it to be zero. … We expect it to be a single-digit percentage of our traffic."
- **Also reported:** Andy Meek, "Google Search AI Overhaul Leaves Publishers Bracing For 'Google Zero'", Forbes, 25 May 2026 — https://www.forbes.com/sites/andymeek/2026/05/25/google-search-ai-overhaul-leaves-publishers-bracing-for-google-zero/ (blocked to our fetcher; quoted via search results).
- **Wording rule:** "Google Zero" is The Verge's term (Nilay Patel), used in headlines — not Lynch's words. Say "plan as if search is zero"; show "Google Zero" only as the name people use for it.
- **Not Condé Nast:** no Condé Nast figure for its current Google share was found. The "65% → high 20s" figure belongs to People Inc. (below).
- Title check: Wikipedia lists Lynch as CEO 2019–2026; he said this while CEO. Don't call him "current CEO".

## 3. People Inc.: Google ~65% → "the high 20s" of its traffic · optional, on screen only
