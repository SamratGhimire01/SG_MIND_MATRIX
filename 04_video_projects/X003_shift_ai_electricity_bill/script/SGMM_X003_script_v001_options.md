## Both
- 485 TWh (2025) and ~950 TWh / ~3% (2030) from the IEA's *Key Questions on Energy and AI* (2026); 2030 = projection → marked forecast, said "about", "almost".
- No per-question energy number (estimates disagree); "every answer runs on electricity" makes no number claim.

- My pick: **A** — the stakes are in the viewer's own question, and the power line lighting under a chat bubble reads in frame 1.
