# X004 · SHIFT · "The Trust Gap" — sources (checked 3 Nov 2026)

[1] **Stack Overflow Developer Survey 2026 — AI** (primary). https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2026/ai · methodology https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2026/methodology
- Fielded 23 Jun – 5 Aug 2026; **30,903 valid responses**, 169 countries. Self-selected survey of developers → say "one survey of about 31,000 developers".
- "The overwhelming majority of respondents now use coding assistants and agents daily (73%)"; "Of the over 70% of respondents who use AI daily, 31% use it over four hours per day." → we say **"more than 7 in 10 use AI every day"**.
- "When asked when it's acceptable to trust AI, **48%** indicate they trust AI when they can easily validate the answers … and only **16%** extend that trust to most tasks that are not important work decisions." → hero: **16%**; context: 48%.
- "Basically, developers trust AI with machines but not with people." · "human-in-the-loop is the dominant mode of working with these tools."

[2] **Stack Overflow Developer Survey 2025 — AI** (primary, used only in option B). https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2025/ai · fielded 29 May – 23 Jun 2025, 49,009 responses.
- "AI solutions that are almost right, but not quite": **66%** (biggest frustration, n = 31,476).
- Trust accuracy: 33% trust vs 46% distrust (n = 33,244).

Wording rules: "use AI every day" (not "rely on"); 16% = "trust it with most tasks" (the survey's own phrase is "most tasks that are not important work decisions" — shown small on screen); "the next skill may be checking" is our forecast, said with "may".
Not used: Google "25% of new code is AI" (Oct 2024, earnings call) — old and only Google.
