The Matrix Is Online

Storyboard v002 · script enhanced 9:16 · 1080×1920 · 30 fps

It opens in pitch black. One keystroke floods the room green, and its three screens become three times. The camera goes through the left screen into the past, films the present live on the right screen, and goes through the top screen into a 3D field of what comes next. It ends the way it began: a second keystroke, every light cuts out, and in the dark one screen types what's coming. The last black frame loops into the first.
