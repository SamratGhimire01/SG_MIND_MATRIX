Decisions for you

Seven calls before I build

Who is "the character"?

You, faceless: hands, back of head, silhouette. SIGNAL isn't rigged yet, so it can't sit at the desk.

→ recommend: you, faceless
Your 3D scan as the person

The scan you sent ("3d-scan-man-1") is a single static, unrigged mesh: about 176k vertices with 4K textures. It's a real person's scanned face, it shows sneaker logos and a cross necklace, and the licence isn't in the zip. To sit and type, it needs rigging and posing first. It fits a fully 3D desk scene. For filmed footage you are the person, and the scan isn't needed.

→ tell me: filmed studio, or a full 3D studio? And where is the scan from (licence)?
Which screen is the "centre monitor"?

The desk has left (AOC), right (Philips) and top (Himstar). The plan puts the SG mark on the top screen.

→ recommend: top (Himstar)
Can the LED strip chase?

If the remote has a flow/chase mode, the light race is real. If not, I build it in the edit from an off plate and an on plate.

→ either works; tell me which
