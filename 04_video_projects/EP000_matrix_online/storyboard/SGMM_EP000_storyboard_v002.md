## 3D in NEXT (B6)

Per bible §8, 3D sits only in NEXT, as abstract forms in the field: never inside the collage or the titles. Spheres and rings, matte only, in Deep Forest / Signal White, plus **one** Matrix Green form (the "next"). Soft key light from the upper left, a faint rim, depth fog into Void, depth of field on forms passing the lens. The camera dollies forward with a slow drift and roll, then settles toward the green form; on "being written" the forms converge toward the white thread. No chrome, no glow, no bloom. Built in three.js or Blender, rendered at 30 fps, and matched 1:1 to the screen's flow field at the handoff.
