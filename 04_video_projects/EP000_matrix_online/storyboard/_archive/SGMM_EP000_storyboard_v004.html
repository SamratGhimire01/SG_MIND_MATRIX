It opens in pitch black: a real hand presses ENTER and the room floods green. A person sits at the desk, seen from behind, with three screens: THEN on the left, NOW on top, NEXT on the right. We go through the left screen into a paper-collage library, watch the present answer live on the top screen, and go through the right screen into a 3D field. A build log and three cards carry the promise, the SG mark rises out of the dark, and a second keystroke cuts every light.

Script review · v001 → v002

What was weak, and the fix

I kept your structure and timings and fixed where people swipe away, where it says nothing, and where it sounds like every other AI channel. Use the Lines button on the player to compare the two versions.

WhereProblemFix in v002
0:00The first 0.4 s showed an empty black frame. On Shorts the swipe decision happens on frame 1.Pitch black, but frame 1 already reads: the line is on screen and spoken in the dark, the ENTER legend glows faintly, a finger descends. The press comes after the line, and the whole room floods green in 0.2 s.
0:023 seconds with no voice and no information, right where viewers decide to stay.The screens snap on reading THEN · NOW · NEXT. The silent beat becomes the map of the film, and the left word then dissolves into the library.
0:06“Twenty years ago” is 2006, when people already searched online. The library picture would be wrong for the date.“A generation ago… you searched. And you waited.” It's still true, and it rhymes with “today” and “next”.
0:24“Will change… everything” is the most common hype line in AI videos, and the brand rejects hype.“What comes next… is already being written.” It sets up the closing line and the channel message (the world is being rewritten).
0:31“I'm going to document it…” is 5 words over 9 seconds. Dead air at the point where retention drops most.“I'm going to document all of it. Every shift. Every tool. Everything I build, in public.” Three beats, each on a new keystroke.
0:39The ending was a static wide with a logo: no tension and no reason to come back. It also dropped the channel's signature line.A slow push toward the mark, then “The world is being rewritten.” On “rewritten” a second CLACK cuts every light. Silence. One screen flickers on and types EPISODE 01 · SATURDAY. Then the last line, whispered: “Let's build it together.”
loopShorts replay automatically. The old ending jumped from a bright wide to a black start.The last black frame matches the first black frame, so the Short loops without a seam and replays count toward views.
allMotion: moves started and stopped at full speed, hard jumps between shots, a flickering field, jittery hands.Every move eases in and out, and each shot starts where the last one ended. Dissolves only where the camera travels; hard cuts only at the act change (THEN → NOW) and the blackout. Motion trails on fast moves, softer hands, and a field that fades instead of popping.
newNo structure markers inside the acts.Act tags (green-disc style from the brand's titles): 01 THEN · 02 NOW · 03 NEXT. A green streaming cursor on the live answer. A 3D NEXT space with volumetric forms.
keep“This is the Matrix.” can read as a Matrix-movie fan line.Kept, because it's your signature, but never paired with green code rain or a CRT look, and spoken calm and low.
