# SGMM publishing (researched 1 Nov 2026, first used for EP000)

Brand truth rules still apply (`01_brand/SGMM_Brand_Bible.md`): no hype words ("SHOCKING", "INSANE"), no fake numbers, calm documentary tone. Write in Samrat's voice. Deliver a posting kit file next to the export: `09_exports/SGMM_EP###_POSTING_KIT.md` plus an `.srt` timed to the real voice.

## What the research says (consistent across sources; treat exact numbers as estimates)
**YouTube Shorts**
- Most Shorts views come from the Shorts feed, not search, so the first 2 seconds and retention matter most; metadata still helps search and categorisation.
- Title: keyword first, under ~60 characters so it is not cut on phones, specific not vague, **no hashtags in the title**.
- Description: main keyword in the first sentence, 2–5 short sentences, then a call to action, then **3–5 relevant hashtags at the end** (always `#Shorts`). Never more than 15 hashtags in total (YouTube may ignore all of them). No #viral/#trending/#fyp. Never paste an identical description on every video.
- Keywords also count when spoken and shown on screen — say and caption the topic words.
- Tags field: 10–15 tags, topic phrases and spelling variants; low weight but harmless.
- Upload an SRT caption file timed to the voice.

**X**
- Upload video **natively**; posts with YouTube links get less reach — put the link in the **first reply**.
- **0–2 specific hashtags**, never 3+.
- Replies and conversations drive growth: reply to everyone, and spend time replying to mid-size accounts in the niche.
- Pin the launch/best post.

## SGMM templates
**YouTube title formulas** (pick the one that fits, ≤60 chars):
- `AI Is [Changing X]. [Promise].` — e.g. "AI Is Rewriting the World. I'll Document All of It."
- `How AI Changed [X]: Then, Now, Next`
- `You Used to [old way]. Now You [new way]. What's Next?`

**Description skeleton**
```
[Keyword sentence: AI + the topic.] [THEN line.] [NOW line.] [NEXT line.]

SG Mind Matrix documents the AI shift — then, now, next — with honest explainers, practical AI tips and real builds made in public.

New episode every Saturday. Subscribe and join the Matrix.

Script and voice are human. Visuals and music are original. AI tools assist research and production.

#AI #FutureOfAI #[TopicTag] #Shorts
```

**Core hashtag set**: `#AI #FutureOfAI #ArtificialIntelligence #Shorts` + one topic tag (e.g. `#AITools`, `#LearnAI`, `#ClaudeAI`, `#BuildInPublic`). On X: `#AI` (+ one topic tag at most).

**Core tags**: AI, artificial intelligence, future of AI, how AI is changing the world, AI explained, then vs now, AI documentary, AI tools, learn AI, Claude AI, technology, future technology, SG Mind Matrix + 2–3 topic phrases. Do not tag "the matrix" (pulls the movie audience).

**Pinned comment**: one open question that invites a personal answer and promises a use ("The best answers go into the next episode").

**X post**: the script's THEN / NOW / NEXT lines as short separate lines, then the promise, then "This is SG Mind Matrix." + Day count; 0–2 hashtags; YouTube link in the first reply.
