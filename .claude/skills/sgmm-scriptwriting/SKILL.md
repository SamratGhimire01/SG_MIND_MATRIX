# SGMM scriptwriting — retention from first to last (researched 2 Nov 2026)

Samrat's brief: scripts must be **engaging enough that people watch from the first second to the last**. Research below; the rules after it are what we do. Brand truth rules still win (`01_brand/SGMM_Brand_Bible.md`, `sgmm-publishing`): no hype words, no fake numbers, every claim sourced, forecasts marked.

## What the platforms actually reward (evidence levels in brackets)
- **Shorts: the swipe decision comes first.** YouTube shows "Viewed vs. swiped away" (how often people watched instead of swiping) next to average view duration and average % viewed. [YouTube Studio metric; thresholds and weighting are NOT published — numbers like "70%+ viewed" are community estimates.]
- **Replays count.** A Short that loops cleanly can pass 100% average viewed. [practitioner guides; consistent across sources]
- **TikTok's own guidance:** state the proposition in the **first 3 seconds**, hook within the first 6, build suspense or surprise into it, use captions/on-screen text (sound-off viewing), and keep adding transitions/graphics to hold attention. [TikTok for Business, creative best practices]
- **The first minute is where most people leave**, so the opening carries the video. [leaked "How to Succeed in MrBeast Production" guide, 2024; reported, not verified]

## The shape of an SGMM Short (≈20–30 s)
| Block | Time | Job | SHIFT act |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | 0–3 s | Frame 1 *is* the hook (no intro, no logo, no greeting). Topic + tension in one breath. | cold open (dark / Enter) or a NOW flash-forward |
| **Lean-in** | 3–7 s | Why it matters to *you*; set up the old world. | THEN |
| **Turn** | ~7–10 s | "**But** …" — the change. | NOW |
| **Proof** | 10–18 s | One number, big, on screen (+ one escalation). "**So** …" | NOW |
| **Next** | 18–24 s | "**Therefore** …" the consequence / forecast (marked "may"). | NEXT |
| **Loop** | last 2–3 s | The last line is the open question that the first line answers on replay. No CTA. | NEXT → end card |

## Explainer shape (Samrat's references, 3 Nov 2026)
For "how does X work?" videos use the 8-beat formula in `sgmm-video-production/POP_EXPLAINER.md`: everyday hook + "the interesting part?" → counter-intuitive claim ("The secret isn't X… it's Y!") → mechanism in 3–5 steps ("Look," / "Suppose you're…" / "Think…") → visual proof → "This is called…" → honest TWIST → "So X doesn't just A… it B!" → catchphrase. 3–8-word sentences, ~2.3 words/s, one hero word per line. **Samrat decided (3 Nov 2026): explainers are 45–60 s (~105–140 words) and end on the SGMM catchphrase, not a loop** — rules 10–12 below (loop, 20–30 s) don't apply to them.

## Rules
**Hook (first line + first frame together)**
1. Say the topic and the tension in the first sentence; show it in the first frame. A verbal hook without a matching visual doesn't work. [Kallaway; TikTok]
2. Use one of: **stakes for the viewer** ("Every time you don't click… someone loses money"), **contradiction / snapback** (context, then "but", then a turn), **visual evidence first** (show the thing, explain after — Johnny Harris's "visual evidence, then context"), **question with a contradiction in it**.
3. The hook must be true and kept by the video. No "nobody", "everyone", "dead" unless a source says so; "one study" beats overclaiming.

**Body**
4. **Every beat links with "but" or "therefore/so", never "and then".** [Trey Parker & Matt Stone, NYU 2014] Write the link words into the script margin to check.
5. **Something new every 1.5–2 s, seen *and* heard**: a component moves (a box lands, a number counts, a bar drains, a stamp hits) and each move has its own sound (click, tick, slide, thud). A held or static frame is a swipe. `sgmm qc` fails any stretch ≥ 2.5 s with no motion and no sound hit (`08_templates/engine/beats.py` prints the timeline). One idea per line; short, staccato sentences.
6. **Context in small pulses after a visual**, never a block of explanation. [Harris]
7. **One hero number per video**, shown big (the one green element), spoken rounded ("40%"), exact on screen ("−40.2%"), with "one study" / source label.
8. Talk to **you** (the viewer's search, the viewer's click), not "people".
9. Open a loop early (a question or a promise) and close it only at the end.

**Ending**
10. **Loop it**: the last line is the question / first half that the opening line answers or completes on replay. Keep energy and volume the same at the join. [loop guides]
11. **No "follow for more" at the end** — it breaks the loop. The SGMM end card (logo from the dark + "NEXT SHIFT · TOMORROW MORNING") stays silent and short so the replay join still works; the spoken loop is the last voice line.

**Length and pace**
12. 20–30 s, about 2.6–2.9 spoken words per second → **55–75 words**. Captions burned in (most watch muted); caption = voice.

## Before sending a script to Samrat — checklist
- [ ] Frame 1 + line 1 carry topic + tension (would you stop scrolling?)
- [ ] Every line joins the next with *but* / *so* / *therefore*
- [ ] A new visual or sound every 2–3 s (list them in the table)
- [ ] One hero number, sourced, labelled; facts / forecasts / lines marked
- [ ] Last line loops into the first line; no CTA
- [ ] 55–75 words; every line ≤ ~3 s spoken
- [ ] No hype words; nothing the sources don't say
- [ ] Give **2 options** with different hooks so Samrat can choose
