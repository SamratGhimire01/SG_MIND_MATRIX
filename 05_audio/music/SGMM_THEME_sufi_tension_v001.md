Render to WAV: `node 04_video_projects/EP000_matrix_online/edit/animatic/render_score.js out.wav` (OfflineAudioContext, 48 kHz).
Mastered for EP000 at -15 LUFS integrated, -1.5 dBTP.

## Palette (reusable in every episode)
| Element | What it is | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Ney | Breathy reed flute: triangle + sine body, pitched breath noise, glides, late vibrato | The voice. Hijaz on D: D Eb F# G A Bb C |
| Daf | Frame drum: deep "dum", dry "tek", jingle ring | Trance pulse. 6/8: dum . tek dum tek . (eighth = 0.2 s) |
| Drone | Tanpura-like detuned saws D2 A2 D3 through a low-pass | The floor everything sits on |
| Breath choir | Saw voices through "ah" formants (730 / 1100 Hz) | Weight on the big moments |
| Dissonance | D against Eb | Unresolved tension before cuts |
| Heartbeat | Sub thumps 60 -> 40 Hz | The dark before a reveal |
| Silence | `cut()` kills the music bus and its reverb tail | The key "hit" — 0:27.5 and the 0:43.4 blackout |

## Rules
Music sits under the voice; effects stay in front; max ~3 layers at once; every big moment is preceded by a cut to silence.
No samples or third-party recordings are used, so there are no Content ID or licence risks.
