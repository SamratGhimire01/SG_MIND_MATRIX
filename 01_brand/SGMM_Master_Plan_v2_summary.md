# SG Mind Matrix — Master Plan v2 (summary, 31 Oct 2026)

The full plan lives in Samrat's Project. This is the working summary that the repo depends on.

**Identity:** See how the world is changing. Then learn to build with it.
**Promise:** Every video, you understand the world a little better and can do one new thing.
**Atmosphere:** Late night, green glow, calm voice, the world on the screens.

## Signature lines
- Open: "This is the Matrix. Let's see how the world is changing."
- Close: "The world is being rewritten. Let's build it together."
- Recurring device: THEN · NOW · NEXT title cards.

## Pillars
Then · Now · Next (flagship, 10–15 min) · The Week in AI, Explained (6–9 min) · AI Tips (4–8 min + Shorts) · Build With Me (8–12 min) · The Setup (5–8 min + Shorts). One long video every Saturday 7:45 pm Nepal time, three Shorts a week.

## The studio (home base)
Three screens: **AOC left = THEN**, **Philips right = NOW**, **Himstar on the wall above = NEXT**. Green LED wall, RGB keyboard, Mac mini M4. Room lights off; screens are the key light.

## Camera look for real footage
- Green LEDs on, room lights off. Underexpose slightly so screens and glow carry the image.
- Grade: lift blacks slightly, push shadows toward green, keep screen content neutral and readable.
- Calm movement only: slow slides, slow push-ins. No fast pans (one designed exception: the speed-ramped pull-back in EP000).
- Phone: Blackmagic Camera app, lock exposure, white balance and focus, 30 fps.

## Sound
Dark ambient / soft synth bed (reuse 5 tracks so the channel has a sound), soft keyboard clicks, low hum, one deep hit on title cards. Voice: Samrat's own, recorded close and quiet, cleaned with Adobe Podcast Enhance.

## Anonymity check before every shoot
- Remove the college ID lanyard from the room.
- No name on any AI chat screen: brand Chrome profile, blank chat, no history sidebar.
- Replace any wallpaper with another creator's watermark ("JK") — use SGMM THEN / NOW / NEXT wallpapers.
- Hide the bookmarks bar (Cmd+Shift+B) and close personal tabs.
- Keep trademarked figures (Batman, Deadpool) out of hero shots and thumbnails. Check for mail, documents, certificates, identifiable window views.

## Tools
DaVinci Resolve 21 (main edit) · CapCut (Shorts) · OBS (screen) · Blackmagic Camera (phone) · Audacity + Adobe Podcast Enhance (voice) · Figma · HandBrake.
