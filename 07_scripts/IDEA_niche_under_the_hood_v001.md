# NICHE — "Under the Hood": how AI actually works inside (brainstorm v001, 3 Nov 2026)

Samrat's idea: people know AI from the surface; show them what happens *inside* — how ChatGPT answers a prompt, how a self-driving car sees, etc. A niche with no ceiling on topics.

## Why it has no limit
Every AI product, feature and news story is an episode: "How X actually works". New tools ship every week = new episodes every week. Formula: THEN (how it was done before AI, rules/humans) → NOW (the mechanism, 3–5 steps) → NEXT (where it's going / its limit).

## Research (directional; verify)
- Visual AI explainers exist but are mostly long and math-heavy (3Blue1Brown, StatQuest, Two Minute Papers). Few make short, cinematic, everyday-product mechanism videos. https://www.buildfastwithai.com/best/youtube-channels-to-learn-ai
- AI explainers called a fast-growing but competitive sub-niche (vendor claim). https://clippie.ai/blog/faceless-ai-explainer-channel-youtube-science-tech-2026
- Money: Shorts RPM is tiny (~$0.01–0.50, shared pool); long-form education ~$8–14 RPM (vendor estimates). So Shorts = reach, Saturday long-form deep dive = revenue. https://shortsfast.com/blog/faceless-youtube-earnings-2026/ · https://blog.autonolab.com/niches/2025-11-13-faceless-youtube-educational/
- Visual motion-graphic explanation = high retention + high saves (vendor claims).

## The hard part
Accuracy. Every mechanism must be checked against a primary source (papers, official engineering blogs). Simplify, never falsify; say "simplified" on screen when we compress.

## Episode bank (first 24)
Talking AI: what happens after you press Enter (tokens → next-word prediction) · why AI "hallucinates" · how AI remembers a conversation (context window) · how AI reads a photo · how voice assistants hear you · how AI translates.

Feeds: how the YouTube/TikTok feed picks your next video · how spam filters work · how Netflix/Spotify recommend.
Making AI: how an image generator turns noise into a picture (diffusion) · how AI video is made · how voice cloning works · how AI music is made.
World AI: how Google Maps predicts traffic · how banks catch card fraud · how weather AI forecasts · how AlphaFold folds proteins.
Building AI: how AI is trained (data → loss → adjust) · what a GPU does · how much electricity one AI answer uses.
