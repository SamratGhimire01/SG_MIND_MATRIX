## Phase 1 — NOW: 3 videos every day, nothing else new
Make the daily trio reliable before adding anything:
1. **SHIFT** (morning) — one thing AI changed: Then → Now → Next (like X001–X003).
2. **UNDER THE HOOD** (afternoon, takes the TIP slot) — how one AI thing works inside, 3–5 steps, Manim style. First: "What happens in the 2 seconds after you press Enter".
3. **LOG** (evening) — build-in-public: what we built/learned today, real screen capture + cards.
Rules: reuse what works (X001–X003 covers/thumbnail system, Signal Mix, scene library, posting kits); batch scripts on Sunday; render a day ahead; every gate in `sgmm-quality-gates` green before sending.
Exit criterion: 7 days in a row of 3 videos/day shipped on time.

## Phase 2 — after Phase 1 is steady
- Saturday long-form: "Under the Hood" deep dive (8–15 min), segments rendered + CapCut assembly.
- Add the other styles one at a time (Collage THEN, Voxel/Flow Field NEXT, Papercraft/Claymation for explainers).
- Learn from analytics weekly (`06_research/analytics.md`); double down on what retains.

## Phase 3 — later
- SGMM Studio app (`07_scripts/IDEA_SGMM_Studio_app.md`): research, scheduling, analytics, comments.
- Optional series: "Earn With AI (Honest)" (`07_scripts/IDEA_series_earn_with_AI_v001.md`).
