- Upwork In-Demand Skills 2026 (company-reported): demand for AI-applied skills +109% YoY; AI video generation/editing +329%; traditional skills still strong. https://investors.upwork.com/news-releases/news-release-details/upworks-demand-skills-2026-demand-top-ai-skills-more-doubles-ai

- Nepal: Upwork/Fiverr pay out via Payoneer to local banks (~2% conversion per one guide; verify). Fiverr fee 20%, Upwork ~10% (verify on official sites).

## SGMM's edge
Everyone else sells dreams. SGMM shows **real tests with real numbers, including failures** — "I tried it so you don't have to". No income promises, no screenshots we didn't make, every claim sourced, disclose tools.

## Series options
1. **"AI Gig Test"** — each episode Samrat really tries one AI-powered paid skill for 7 days and logs what happened (hours, cost, earnings — even $0). LOG format on X daily, recap episode Saturday.
2. **"The Skill, Then/Now/Next"** — one paid skill per episode (writing, video editing, data entry, translation, design): how it was done THEN, how AI does it NOW, what clients will pay for NEXT.
3. **"60-Second AI Tip that saves a freelancer an hour"** — pure TIP format, one tool trick per day.
4. **"Which jobs AI is creating"** — explainers on the new paid roles (AI video editor, automation builder, AI data trainer, prompt QA) with real demand data.
